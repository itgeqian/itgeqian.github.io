议论文

开头 Living in an era of rapid technological development ,and continuous expansion of knowledge,(主题)have/has become increasingly important,which is significantly impacting ( 某人的生活/学校生活/个人经历 ) 生活在技术快速发展、知识不断扩展的时代，sth已经变得越来越重要，这对影响了某人的生活/学校生活/个人经历

替换版：With technology advancing rapidly and knowledge expanding continuously, (主题) is becoming increasingly important, influencing (某人的生活/学校生活/个人经历) in significant ways. 生活在技术快速发展、知识不断扩展的时代，sth已经变得越来越重要，这对影响了某人的生活/学校生活/个人经历

  • 当主题是…的影响时： Therefore, it is crucial for sb to do sth, and the arguments presented below explain why this view is well-founded

    • 因此，对于某人来说做某事至关重要，下面提出的论点解释了为什么这一观点有充分依据。

  • 当主题是…的重要性时：Therefore,I deem that the (importance of AI …的重要性/要贴近主题) lies in the following aspects

    因此，我认为sth的重要性在于以下几个方面

    • 替换版：Thus, I consider the importance of sth to be evident in the following aspects.
    • 因此，我认为某事的重要性在以下几个方面显而易见。

论据+论点

  • 论点1：First and foremost,there is no doubt that （sth can enhance sb work/study efficiency,and offer tremendous convenience in our daily/school life）

    首先，毫无疑问（某物可以提高某人的工作/学习效率，为我们的日常/学校生活提供极大的便利）

    • 替换: First of all, it is undeniable that (sth can improve sb’s work/study performance, while providing significant convenience in our everyday/school routines). 首先，不可否认的是（这些可以提升SB的工作和学习表现，同时为我们的日常和学校生活提供显著便利）.

  • 论据1：Based on the investigation of big data(Based on big data research), it can be found that，a majority of (Accomplished individuals) admitted that they’ve spent most of their time doing sth

    通过大数据调查可以发现，大多数（成功人士）承认，他们大部分时间都花在做某事上

    • 替换:Studies based on big data suggest that many (well-established professionals) have revealed they dedicate a large portion of their time to sth. 基于大数据的研究表明，许多（资深专业人士）透露他们将大量时间投入到某些事情上

  • 论点2：Moreover,it has formed a consensus that（it has become a general consensus） (by sth can acquire adequate knowledge and experience easily,it is an excellent way for sb to understand what they hope and need)

    而且，这已经形成了一个共识，即（通过某事可以轻松获得足够的知识和经验，这是让某人了解他们希望和需要什么的绝佳方式）

    • 替换：Furthermore, it is widely acknowledged that (sth enables individuals to quickly acquire the necessary knowledge and skills, serving as a perfect tool for sb to comprehend their needs and aspirations). 此外，广泛认可的是，（sth使个人能够快速获得必要的知识和技能，是sb理解其需求和抱负的完美工具）。

  • 论点3：Last but not least,I sincerely believe that(by sth can facilitate communication and collaboration,which helpful

    in broadening

    ours horizons)

    最后但同样重要的一点是，我真诚地相信（通过某事可以促进沟通和合作，有助于拓宽我们的视野）

    • 替换:Finally, it is my strong belief that (sth enhances interaction and teamwork, which is instrumental in widening our outlook). 最后，我坚信（这能增强互动和团队合作，这对拓宽我们的视野至关重要）。

结尾

  • In short, the above statement can lead us reach a conclusion

    简而言之，通过上述陈述我们可以得出结论

    • 替换：In summary, the points discussed above bring us to the conclusion that… 总之，上述讨论的观点引出了结论…

  • sth is revolutionizing the way we live,it is predictable that if sb makes reasonable use of sth,the future of sth will be become more beautiful and hopeful

    某事物正在彻底改变我们每个人的生活方式，可以预见，如果某人合理地利用某事物，其未来将会变得更加美好和充满希望

    • 替换：Sth is transforming our way of life, and it is foreseeable that if sb utilizes sth wisely, its future will be more beautiful and hopeful 这正在改变我们的生活方式，可以预见如果SB明智地利用它，其未来将更加乐观和乐观。
